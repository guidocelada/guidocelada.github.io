## Product

Laboratorio Celada is a family-owned clinical laboratory located in La Plata, Argentina, serving patients for more than 30 years from the same location.

The website's primary purpose is not marketing. It is to help patients quickly find practical information before visiting the laboratory.

The website should prioritize trust, clarity and accessibility over visual novelty.

---

## Target Audience

### Primary

Adults aged 50-80 years old.

Typical users want to know:

- Opening hours
- Address
- How to prepare for a test
- Whether fasting is required
- Accepted health insurance providers
- How to receive results
- How to contact the laboratory

### Secondary

Family members helping elderly relatives.

---

## Business Goals

1. Increase trust.
2. Increase WhatsApp inquiries.
3. Reduce repetitive phone calls.
4. Improve local search visibility.
5. Clearly communicate available services.

---

## Services

- Clinical laboratory studies
- Blood extraction
- Home blood extraction
- Results delivery by email
- WhatsApp support

---

## Key Trust Signals

The design should prominently communicate:

- More than 30 years serving La Plata
- Same location for decades
- Google Maps reviews
- More than 10,000 patients served every year
- Personalized professional attention
- Results delivered by email
- Home blood extraction service

---

## Website Goals

A visitor should be able to find the following information within 5 seconds:

- Address
- Opening hours
- WhatsApp contact
- Home blood extraction availability
- Results delivery process

---

## Design Principles

- Mobile-first
- Accessibility-first
- Simple navigation
- Minimal cognitive load
- Large typography
- Large touch targets
- High contrast
- Fast loading
- Real photography
- No unnecessary animations

The website should feel:

- Trustworthy
- Professional
- Established
- Local
- Human

The website should NOT feel:

- Like a startup
- Like a SaaS landing page
- Corporate
- Over-designed
- Trendy

---

## Brand

Primary accent color:

- Warm yellow derived from the existing brand identity

Usage:

- Buttons
- Highlights
- Icons
- Trust indicators

Do not use yellow as the primary background color.

Primary UI:

- White backgrounds
- Dark typography
- Yellow accents

Target WCAG 2.2 AA compliance.

Requirements:

- Minimum 16px body text
- Large tap targets
- Keyboard navigation
- Proper heading hierarchy
- High contrast
- Readable forms
- Screen reader support

## Performance Requirements

- Accessibility: 100
- Best Practices: 100
- SEO: 100

## Success Criteria

The redesign is successful if users can immediately answer:

1. Where is the laboratory?
2. What are the opening hours?
3. How do I contact the laboratory?
4. How do I receive results?
5. Do they offer home blood extraction?
6. Does my insurance provider work there?

without searching or navigating multiple pages.
